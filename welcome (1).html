A MESSAGE TO FELLOW LEARNERS

You do not have to know everything today. What matters is your willingness to learn, ask questions and keep improving.


Be curious. Investigate. Make mistakes. Learn from them. Create something useful. Then help someone else understand what you have discovered.

ACADEMIC ADVICE

"Do not study only to remember. Study to understand."

When you understand why something happens, you are more capable of explaining it, applying it to new situations and remembering it for longer. Make understanding your goal, then use revision, practice and questions to strengthen what you have learned.
